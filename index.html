Notes

Definitions
  • Refused: the site returned an HTTP 403 or 429, or a bot-challenge page, to our labeled agent; cause not established.
  • Published rules: robots.txt names AI agents or crawlers (allow or disallow), or has a Disallow group for *.
  • Error: timeout or network error, counted as an error, not a refusal.
  • Not refused: the checked files and homepage loaded normally.
Method

Sites = NRF Top 100 Retailers 2026 (ranked by US retail sales), grouped by primary category; niches under 10 merged; one company excluded. A logged-out check of public policy files (robots.txt, llms.txt, /.well-known) and the homepage response, aggregated by category. n=83, Oct 31 and Nov 1, 2026. Dated observations, not statements of intent.

What we tested and its limits
  • One labeled bot. Every request used one honest user agent that names this project and links to it. No browser disguise, logins, cookies or retries around a refusal.
  • One run per check date, one request per endpoint. Each site got at most one request each to robots.txt, llms.txt, a few /.well-known files and the homepage (plus at most one retry after a timeout), capped at 8 requests, 2 seconds apart.
  • robots.txt respected. Any path robots.txt disallowed for our bot was skipped. On the first refusal we stopped checking that site.
  • One vantage point. All checks ran from a single cloud network. Sites may answer other networks, regions or real shoppers differently.
  • Coverage. 83 of the NRF Top 100. The rest were not covered, including one company excluded over a potential conflict of interest.
  • What "refused" covers. An HTTP 403 or 429 response, or a bot-challenge or CAPTCHA page, on any checked file. Timeouts and network errors count as errors, not refusals. We did not establish why a site refused.
  • Stable across two check dates. Oct 31 and Nov 1 gave the same results for every site, from one vantage point.
  • ACP not measured. ACP has no settled public discovery file; ACP checkout runs through agent-platform integrations not visible externally, so it was not measured. 0 sites served the proposed acp.json.
  • No ranking. Results are aggregated by category. We do not rank or name retailers.
