Notes

Definitions
  • Explicit disallow: robots.txt has Disallow groups for AI agents or crawlers (or *).
  • Allowed or OK: AI user agents named and allowed, or no policy and the fetch succeeded.
  • Refused, no policy: no published AI-agent rules, and the site returned an access error or challenge page. Cause not established.
  • Error: timeout or network error, recorded as an error, not a refusal.
Method

A logged-out daily check of public policy files (robots.txt, llms.txt, /.well-known) and the homepage response for 160 hand-picked retail and commerce sites, aggregated by category. n=160, Oct 31, 2026. Dated observations, not statements of intent.
