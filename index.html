Notes

Definitions
  • Refused: the site returned an access error or challenge page to a labeled bot; cause not established. Policy: published rules for AI agents.
  • Explicit disallow: robots.txt has Disallow groups for AI agents or crawlers (or *).
  • Allowed or OK: AI user agents named and allowed, or no policy and the fetch succeeded.
  • Refused, no policy: no published AI-agent rules, and the site returned an access error or challenge page. Cause not established.
  • Error: timeout or network error, recorded as an error, not a refusal.
Method

Sites = NRF Top 100 Retailers 2026 (ranked by US retail sales), grouped by primary category; niches under 10 merged; one company excluded. A logged-out check of public policy files (robots.txt, llms.txt, /.well-known) and the homepage response, aggregated by category. n=83, Oct 31, 2026. Dated observations, not statements of intent.
