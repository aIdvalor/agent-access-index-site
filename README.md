# Agent Access Index

A category-level index of how retail and commerce sites treat AI agents. Logged-out daily check of public policy files, n=83 sites from the NRF Top 100 Retailers 2026, Oct 31, 2026.

**Finding:** most top US retailers that refuse AI agents publish no policy on them. 25 of 29 refusals (86%) came from sites with no AI-agent rules at the URLs checked.

Dashboard: https://aidvalor.github.io/agent-access-index-site/
