# Agent Access Index

A category-level index of how retail and commerce sites treat AI agents. Logged-out weekly check of public policy files, n=83 sites from the NRF Top 100 Retailers 2026, checked 3 dates, Oct 31 to Nov 2, 2026.

**Finding:** 84% of top US retailers (70 of 83) publish no AI-agent rules. 35% (29 of 83) refused our labeled agent.

Dashboard: https://aidvalor.github.io/agent-access-index-site/
